    How to use

    Click empty map: add a junction, its road coming from the selected one (the garage at first).
    Click a junction: select it. Drag it to move.
    Right-drag or middle-drag: pan. Wheel: zoom.
    Connect to…: then click the junction its road should come from.
    Add road from…: another junction's road also leads here, so roads merge (one way only, no loops).
    Del delete (what's beyond moves onto the one before) · Ctrl+Z undo · Esc cancel / select the garage.

    A junction's number (JCT 1, 2, 3...) is how many junctions along the road from the garage it is: deeper means harder (gateway cost, anchors, storms), and the story's goals go by it (escape at JCT 3, 6, 9). Its zone (colour) is the ring area it sits in: Outer, Mid or Deep (the solid rings are the borders), which sets its look, robots and when it unlocks.

    Anything left on Random is rolled by the game as usual. Past a junction with no roads on (unless it's a Dead end) the map goes on as a random one.

    A save made before the file existed keeps its random map: start a new save. Clear the game's Cache folder after saving.
